NOTE ON THE HOUSEHOLD SURVEY

The survey data distributed here have been de-identified. Respondent names,
phone numbers, GPS coordinates, enumerator identifiers, interview timestamps,
village names and all open-ended free-text responses have been removed, and
villages are identified by anonymous numeric codes only. This licence covers
the de-identified data as distributed.

Do not attempt to re-identify individual respondents or the study villages,
whether by linkage to other datasets or by any other means. The identified
source data are not public; researchers with a legitimate need may request
access from the corresponding author under a confidentiality agreement.
